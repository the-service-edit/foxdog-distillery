  • Your list opens your emails (about 20%, which is healthy). It just hasn't heard from you often: three sends since June, and around 400 people have left the list in that time. A steady weekly rhythm through Christmas keeps Foxdog front of mind.
  • Christmas gifting goes first, on 12 November. It's the email that sells to the whole country, so it needs to land while there's plenty of time to post.
  • A reason to buy now: a free crystal nosing glass with any 700mL bottle or gift pack ordered online, with code SUNNYSIDE, until 30 November. Your call. It's question one below.
  • Your welcome email now sells a first bottle, so every new sign-up gets a reason to order straight away.
  • Every button goes to a real page. Shop orders show up in Mailchimp against each email. Gift cards and bookings are counted from the tracked links.
